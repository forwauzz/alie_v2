# IVAC — what the rules cannot tell you

Read by the orchestrator and the adjudicator, never by an extractor.

Judgement only. Every rule lives in pack data; where this page and the data disagree, the
data is right. Several questions below have no rule at all yet, and the pack says so — an
unanswered question is not an invitation to answer it here.

## The offence date selects the statute

Which act governs follows from when the offence happened, not from when the file was
opened: LIVAC before the change, LAPVIC after it. So the offence date is not one fact
among many — it decides which framework the rest of the file is read under.

And it is often not a single date. Repeated offences over a period are ordinary here, so
treat the offence as a **set or a range**. A model that collapses it to one date discards
most of the claim.

## This file may not be one regime

The gold file is the example: a logger assaulted on his own logging property. The bundle
carries CNESST documents because the workplace statute takes precedence, and those units
belong to the other regime's vocabulary and impairment math.

So when a document here reads as workplace-injury material, that is a routing question, not
a misfiled page. The per-unit screener re-tags what it recognises and records what the
document said to earn it — read that evidence before overriding.

**When precedence actually applies is unresolved in this pack.** It is a legal test and no
rule encodes it. Escalate; do not infer one from the documents.

## Consequences are frequently psychological

The impairment here is often psychiatric rather than orthopaedic. A psychologist's note may
be the central evidence, not a peripheral one, and treating it as minor loses the claim.

## No opinion binds

Like SAAQ and unlike CNESST, there is no binding-treating-opinion tier here.

## What to escalate rather than decide

- **Faute lourde.** The engine flags a mention and never adjudicates it. Whether it
  disqualifies, and to what degree, is a legal test.
- Which rating instrument governs after 2021.
- Any admissibility element the pack marks unresolved.
